隱私權政策 (HorizonPTZ 繁體中文)

最後更新日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日

HorizonPTZ（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）是一套專為 macOS 設計的專業 PTZ 攝影機控制、導播機連線整合、AI 人流辨識輔助與 YouTube 直播即時監控軟體。

我們極度重視您的隱私，並致力於保護您的個人資料。本隱私權政策旨在說明本應用程式如何存取、處理您的資料以及您的權利。

一、 資料收集與處理原則

HorizonPTZ 採用「本地優先（Local-First）」架構。我們未架設任何第三方數據追蹤伺服器，絕不收集、不出售、亦不向任何第三方傳輸您的個人敏感資料。

  • 攝影機影像串流與 AI 辨識：本應用程式直接透過區域網路與您的 PTZ 攝影機建立 RTSP 視訊連線。所有影像辨識與人流統計均透過 Apple 晶片內建之神經網路引擎 (Neural Engine / GPU) 在您的 Mac 本機記憶體中完成即時運算。絕不將任何監視畫面、影格、截圖或特徵資料上傳至雲端或遠端伺服器。
  • 設備連線與網路通訊：您所設定的攝影機 IP、VISCA 連線埠、導播機 IP 等參數，均僅儲存於您本機的 macOS 偏好設定（UserDefaults）中。對攝影機與 RGBlink 導播機的所有控制指令均嚴格限制在您的區域網路（LAN）內傳輸。
  • YouTube 帳號與直播功能整合：若您啟用 YouTube 直播控制台，授權流程直接與 Google 官方驗證伺服器對接。授權憑證（Token）僅保存在您 Mac 的安全儲存區（Keychain），僅用於透過 Google 官方 YouTube Live Streaming API 讀取您當前的直播健康度、觀看人數與即時留言。本應用程式不會將您的 YouTube 資訊轉發至外部。您使用 YouTube 服務時亦受 Google 隱私權政策 規範。

二、 系統權限與沙盒規範 (App Sandbox)

依據 Apple Mac App Store 審查指南，HorizonPTZ 完整運行於 macOS 沙盒（Sandbox）安全架構內，僅請求必要權限：

  • com.apple.security.network.client：用於連線區域網路攝影機、導播機及 YouTube API。
  • com.apple.security.network.server：用於接收導播機本地 Tally/監聽訊號。
  • com.apple.security.keychain：用於在本機加密保存您的 YouTube API 授權凭據。

三、 資料保存與刪除

所有設定資料均保存在您的本機裝置中。您可以隨時在軟體內登出 YouTube，或前往 Google 帳戶安全性設定 撤銷授權；移除本應用程式即可完整清除本機相關設定。

Privacy Policy (HorizonPTZ English)

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

HorizonPTZ ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a macOS desktop controller application designed for professional PTZ camera management, video switcher integration, AI person-counting assistance, and YouTube Live broadcasting monitoring.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application accesses, how it is handled, and your choices.

1. Information Collection and Handling

HorizonPTZ operates as a local-first utility. We do not operate backend tracking servers, and we do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data to any external parties.

  • Camera Video Streams & On-Device AI Processing: The Application connects directly to your local PTZ cameras via RTSP (Real-Time Streaming Protocol). All computer vision and person-counting features (via Apple Vision and CoreML YOLO) are processed entirely locally in your Mac's on-chip Neural Engine / GPU / RAM. No video frames, camera snapshots, or facial data are ever uploaded to any cloud or remote servers.
  • Hardware Settings & Local Network: Camera IP addresses, VISCA/ONVIF ports, and video switcher connection details are stored strictly in your local macOS preferences (UserDefaults). Network requests to your hardware remain strictly within your Local Area Network (LAN).
  • YouTube Account & Live Streaming Integration: If you connect your YouTube Studio account, authentication is performed directly with Google's OAuth 2.0 service. Access and refresh tokens are stored securely on your local device (in macOS Keychain) solely to fetch stream health metrics, viewer counts, and chat messages via the official Google YouTube Live Streaming API. Your usage of YouTube services is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

2. System Permissions & App Sandbox

In accordance with Apple Mac App Store guidelines, HorizonPTZ runs within the macOS App Sandbox and requests only minimal necessary entitlements:

  • com.apple.security.network.client: Required to connect to local PTZ cameras, video switchers, and the YouTube API.
  • com.apple.security.network.server: Required for local tally feedback / port listener integration with video switchers.
  • com.apple.security.keychain: Required for encrypting and securely retaining your YouTube API authentication tokens.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All application configurations are stored locally on your device. You can disconnect your YouTube account at any time in the app or via Google Account Permissions. Removing the app completely deletes local preferences.
